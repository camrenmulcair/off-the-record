Blockade, Meet the Jerry Can: The US Navy vs. Iran's Pakistan Back Door

14 tankers are massing at Kharg Island, and Tehran says the first shot puts the Gulf's oil in play.

TEHRAN — CENTCOM's new Task Force Iron Gate is going after Iran's land borders and Caspian routes, on top of the naval blockade of Iranian ports in place since April 13. Iran's back door is already open: traders told RFE/RL that at least 6 million litres a day of Iranian fuel crosses into Pakistan in canisters on pickups and motorcycles, with boats running more to Gwadar. That's about 38,000 barrels a day, millions of barrels since the war began. Now 14+ tankers are massing at Kharg Island, Deputy FM Kazem Gharibabadi calls the blockade an "act of war," Iranian TV is floating putting journalists and civilians aboard, and an Iranian official warns that "if a single shot is fired," Gulf oil facilities become targets. Iran is betting the US won't sink a tanker full of civilians on live TV, and that the jerry cans keep flowing to world markets while Washington makes up its mind. I think it's right on both.

Britain Says the Quiet Part Out Loud: We're Hacking Russia, China, Iran and North Korea

Asked if it's really happening, Britain's top cyber general said: "Absolutely."

LONDON — Gen. The Oil War Goes Global: From the Baltic to Hormuz, One Fleet, Two Wars

Ukraine's drones reach Libya and the Baltic. Mines reach Hormuz. Same oil, same ships.

BENGHAZI, LIBYA — On Nov. 21–22, Ukraine hit the Russian corvette Merkury and the sanctioned cargo ship Angara off Benghazi, and the Defence Ministry-owned Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland. 301 ships sit on both the US Russia and Iran sanctions lists. The tanker Louise 1 shows how it works: it hauled Russian crude, took on 4.65 million barrels of Iranian oil in 2024, got hit by Ukrainian drones in the Black Sea in July, then by limpet mines near Hormuz on Oct. 29. Iran now says three more tankers hit mines, yet Brent sits near a three-month low around $89. Russia and Iran sell the same oil on the same ships. It's one war over one global commodity, fought on every ocean, and Ukraine has figured that out.

Nuke the Mountain, Lose the War

Trump is eyeing Pickaxe Mountain. Not even a nuclear strike wins a war he's already losing.

Doha talks collapsed, and Trump says Pickaxe Mountain, Iran's deepest nuclear site, is on the table. On Oct 29, Sen. Chris Murphy, who had been briefed on the plan, went public: restarting the war after the midterms is "insane." Two days later, the 53-year-old had a stroke at his son's hockey game. Coincidence? I have my doubts. Either way, no bomb wins this. Winning means a ground war needing more boots than Iraq and Afghanistan combined (roughly 270,000 at their peaks), and that means a full national draft. Officially, 23 US troops have been killed. I suspect the real casualty count is closer to 200.
