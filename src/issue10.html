CNN Warned You About Iran's Dirty Bomb. Then the Army Set One Off in Iowa.

Five tons of toxic explosive, a town evacuated, and a cold snap that forced the Army's hand.

MIDDLETOWN, IOWA — On Nov. 16, CNN reported that US intelligence says Iran is developing plans to set off a "dirty bomb or a chemical weapon" in Washington. Nine days later, at about 9 a.m. on Nov. 25, the Army blew up about 5 tons of degraded lead azide, a toxic, lead-based explosive, at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant. Middletown and a 5-mile zone were told to evacuate, people within 20 miles who stayed were told to shelter in place, and the governor declared a disaster in 3 counties. The Army says the stockpile had turned unstable, and the Justice Department told a court that cold weather "dramatically increases the risk of an uncontrolled explosion." Acting Army Secretary Adam Telle called it a "huge success." They let it sit until winter turned it into a bomb, then set off a dirty explosion in the heartland while CNN had everyone looking at Iran. Who cares if Tehran's plot is "imminent"? The one that actually went off was ours.

The Password Manager Lost Its Keys: One GitHub Account to Breach Them All

Your vault is safe, 1Password says. Your name, your email and its source code aren't.

TORONTO — On Nov. 25, Toronto-based 1Password said an attacker used a compromised employee GitHub account to get into its internal backend systems and export customer names, email addresses, account and user IDs and security tokens, plus the company's source code. 1Password says vaults, private keys and recovery keys weren't touched, and it has found no account takeovers. Login codes sent by email are now nine characters instead of six. The company that sells you one key to everything got opened with one employee's login. Change your master password and expect some very convincing phishing.

The DNC Discovers Money in Politics

Want on the 2028 debate stage? Denounce your super PAC first. Whatcha gonna do, Gavin?

WASHINGTON — On Nov. 23, the DNC announced 12 primary debates for 2028, starting in June 2027, and a "Fair Campaign Practices Pledge": to get on stage, candidates must denounce super PAC and dark-money spending on their behalf. The DNC admits it has no legal power to ban super PACs, so it will publish a tracker of complaints and denunciations. Bernie Sanders called it "excellent." A spokesman for Maryland Gov. Wes Moore called it "virtue signal[ing]" that could "cost us elections." Gavin Newsom says he'll decide on a run in January. Whatcha gonna do, Gavin? Every big-money Democrat just got told to pick between the donors and the debate stage.

FBI, USDA, CIA, Now Treasury: The Agency That Tracks Dirty Money Just Got Hacked

FinCEN pulled systems offline. Nobody's saying who did it.

WASHINGTON — On Nov. 25, FinCEN, the Treasury bureau that tracks money laundering, said it's investigating "unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems" and took some of them offline. The FBI and CISA are helping, and officials haven't said who did it or what was taken. A day earlier, the CIA and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence reported a breach of an open-source analysis system, a week after hackers hit the FBI's crime database and the USDA. That's four federal agencies in two weeks. At this point it's easier to list who hasn't been hacked.

False Flag Rehearsal Over Flushing Bay? LaGuardia's Second Shoulder-Fired Missile (MANPADS) Scare

The tower warned pilots of a missile launcher by the runway. Police found nothing. Again.

NEW YORK — Around 6:30 p.m. on Nov. 25, LaGuardia's tower warned pilots of a possible MANPADS, a shoulder-fired anti-aircraft missile launcher, just east of the airport near Flushing Bay, and told them to "exercise extreme caution." Port Authority police searched and found nothing, and the alert was called not credible. It's the second such alert since July 22, when a phoned-in tip set off a warning to a Delta flight on final approach. The FAA listed no ground stop, and no agency has said a word publicly. Two phantom missile launchers at the same airport in four months, the same day as Iowa and the week CNN is selling an Iranian plot. Somebody is testing how fast New York panics.

He got McCarthy the job. Then he told Pat McAfee the man snores through meetings.

Gone for Half a Century: 130 Orphans Later, Argentina Gets Its Giant Anteaters Back

Hunted out of Corrientes last century, now settled for good in Iberá.

Guest writer: Yuri

CORRIENTES, ARGENTINA — The giant anteater disappeared from Corrientes province by the mid-20th century, wiped out by poaching, road deaths and land cleared for farms. Since 2007, Fundación Rewilding Argentina and the provincial government have rescued more than 130 orphans whose mothers were hunted in the north, raised them, fitted them with satellite collars and released them in Iberá National Park. Researchers now say the animals have adapted and settled for good, and Iberá's anteaters number about 200, descendants included. An animal people erased from a whole province is back, raised one orphan at a time. That's what good news looks like.
