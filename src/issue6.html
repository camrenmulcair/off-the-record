The Deep South Cracks: Colom Takes Mississippi

The AP has called it. Mississippi went blue. Eventually.

JACKSON, MS — Democrat Scott Colom beat Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith 49.2% to 48.7%, a margin of a few thousand votes. It's the first time Mississippi has sent a Democrat to the Senate in more than 40 years, and the party's eighth Senate flip of the cycle, powered by a surge in Black turnout. If Mississippi can flip, no seat in the South is safe.

DC's Doomsday: Gunn and Safran Out, Marvel Man In

A new owner, a new boss, and a DC Universe in limbo.

Guest writer: Willy

BURBANK, CA — On Monday, it was announced that James Gunn and Peter Safran are OUT as the heads of DC Studios following Paramount Skydance’s recent acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery. Taking their place is former Marvel Studios producer Jeremy Latcham, who previously worked on films such as Iron Man, The Avengers, and Guardians of the Galaxy. Their departure leaves the future of the DC Universe uncertain, as several projects currently in development could face significant changes or potentially never see the light of day.
