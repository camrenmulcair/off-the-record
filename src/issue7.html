The Sky Shut for Four Hours and They Blamed a Copper Wire

The second nationwide ground stop since 9/11, and the official villain is a router.

WASHINGTON — The FAA halted every departure in the country for more than four hours on Friday afternoon, disrupting over 10,000 flights. Officials blame an L3Harris upgrade on an old copper line that overloaded air traffic routers, and Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin says there was no cyberattack. The FAA has now paused router installations. Nobody does infrastructure work on a busy Friday. I think this was a coordinated cyberattack on the new systems the FAA is rolling in to replace its floppy disks. Floppies can't be hacked, and from what I've heard, the controllers' union didn't want them gone.
