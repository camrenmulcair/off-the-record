The Agency That Hears Everything Can't Hear Itself. NSA Still Down 3 Days Later, Mid-War.

"Just an update," they say.

FORT MEADE, MD — NSANet, the agency's classified internal network, went down on Tuesday, and the outage is still ongoing three days later. Officials rule out a cyberattack and point to security patches for flaws found with the help of AI. Three days of "maintenance" at the world's biggest spy agency, during a war? This was a cyber attack, and the official story is cover.

Hydro-Québec Discovers Two-Factor Authentication, 2026

2,500 accounts broken into, 1.7 million customers handed the homework.

MONTREAL — On Oct. 20, attackers got into more than 2,500 Hydro-Québec online customer accounts using emails and passwords stolen from other sites. Hydro says its internal systems weren't compromised and no full bank account or social insurance numbers leaked. Now 1.7 million of its 2.8 million customers have to reset their passwords, and a security code by email has been required to log in since Nov. 5. A utility this big only just turned on two-factor login. Welcome to 2026, Hydro.

EU Commission Confirms AI-Powered Hack: Bureaucrats Locked Out of Their Own Payslips for 10 Days

The people who wrote the rules on AI just got breached by it.

BRUSSELS — The European Commission confirmed on Thursday that it was targeted using ARTEX, a publicly available AI hacking tool. Sysper, the HR system staff use for payslips, leave and timesheets, and the medical reimbursement system were knocked offline for about 10 days. The Commission says there was no ransom demand, won't say who did it, and is still checking whether staff names, pay grades and home addresses were taken. Anyone can download the tool that just breached the EU's executive. That's the scary part.

iCloud Goes Dark the Same Week Hackers Hunt iPhones. Apple Swears It Was Just an Update. Sure, Jan.

Apple Pay, iCloud and the App Store, all down on the same afternoon.

CUPERTINO, CA — On Tuesday, iCloud, Apple Pay, the App Store and Apple Music stopped working, with nearly 2,300 Apple Pay reports on Downdetector at the peak. Hackers spend the week going after iPhones, then Apple's whole cloud falls over and we're told it's a coincidence. I think Apple got hit, and the "update" story is cover.

Canada Is the First Country to Shop for Carbon Removal

Ottawa will buy credits from 19 Canadian projects that pull CO₂ out of the air.

Guest writer: Milosz

OTTAWA — Treasury Board President Shafqat Ali announced on Thursday that federal departments can now buy permanent carbon dioxide removal credits from 19 projects across Canada. Ottawa says it's the first national government in the world to set up competitive standing offers for these credits. The projects pull CO₂ out of the atmosphere and store it underground or in long-lasting products, and the credits will cover emissions that are hard to cut, like National Defence's air and marine fleets. It builds on a promise to buy at least $10 million in removal credits by 2030. For once, Canada is first at something that matters. Good.
